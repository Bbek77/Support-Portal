Frequently asked questions.

Some helpful information to get you started.

How can I check my order status?

Sign in to your account and open the Orders page to check your order status. If you still need assistance, contact our support team.

How do I contact customer support?

Email support@example.com. Include a description of your question and your order number when relevant.

When is customer support available?

Our listed support hours are Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central Time.

What should I include in my email?

Describe your issue clearly and include your order number if your question is about an order. Avoid sending passwords or other sensitive account information.
